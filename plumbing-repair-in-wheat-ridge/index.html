What a repair visit may cover

A typical repair process starts with your description of the problem and an inspection to identify its source. The plumber can then explain the repair options and what work is involved. Timing varies: a straightforward repair may take less time than one requiring additional diagnosis, access to a hidden pipe, or a part that isn’t on hand.
